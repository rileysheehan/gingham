- A household can give the wall a page of its own: hold the clock for three seconds and it opens full screen.
- That page is set up by hand on the server, and it does nothing until a household adds one. Settings is unchanged.
- In the Android app, the page opens on its own, with no access to the wall's controls, and only its own site.
- After a few minutes untouched, or on Back, the app brings the wall straight back, still signed in.

Calendars, lists, photos and settings from 0.1.3 carry over as they are. Nothing on the wall changes unless your
household sets up a page.
