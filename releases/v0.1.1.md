- Settings shows which Gingham is running and says when a newer one is out. In the Android app, Install updates it with one tap.
- Setting up needs no typing: every screen that asks for a phone shows a QR code of the whole link.
- Today holds about twice as many things, with what is next and the oldest overdue chores first.
- The clock is 12- or 24-hour, as your country writes it or as you choose in Settings.
- If the calendar or the lists stop updating, the wall says since when, in every view.
- Easier to read from across the room at dawn and dusk.

The update check asks GitHub once a day whether a newer Gingham exists and sends nothing about your household; turn
it off in Settings, under Updates. Calendars, lists, photos and settings from 0.1.0 carry over as they are.
