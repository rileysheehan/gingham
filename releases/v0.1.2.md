- The calendar can be a week or an agenda: one list of the next four weeks, day by day. Choose it in Settings, under Appearance.
- Beside the month, this week’s days are crossed off as they pass, today is circled, and a star marks a day you’re counting down to.
- Clearer titles: the month and each list’s name are bigger, and today’s date is bolder.
- The whole screen turns dark or light together, at dawn and at dusk.

Calendars, lists, photos and settings from 0.1.1 carry over as they are. The calendar stays a week until you choose
Agenda.

Two smaller fixes: under Today, a chore with no time sits right beside its circle unless there's a time to line it up
with, and a household with many calendars gets a smaller legend instead of one that spills out of the header.
