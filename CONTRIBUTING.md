# Contributing

Thank you for looking. Gingham is small on purpose: plain Node with no dependencies and no build step, pages written
for the old WebView that cheap frames ship with.

- `npm run check && npm test` before sending a change. A change to behaviour comes with a test.
- Keep it dependency-free. If something seems to need a library, open an issue first.
- The frame runs on a 1 GB tablet. No CSS animation on the wall: the frame moves its sky once a second instead, and
  that is the budget.
- No real people in fixtures, tests or screenshots. The sample household is the Ashbys (Mara, Theo, June and
  Grandma Nell).
- Words on screen are short, plain and specific, in sentence case.

By contributing you agree your work is released under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE).
