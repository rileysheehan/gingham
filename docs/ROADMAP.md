# Roadmap

Where Gingham is going, roughly in order. Nothing here is promised by a date.

## Next

- **Releases.** Signed Android builds and a container image published from this repository.
- **A guide for newcomers**, including a hardware guide: which tablets and frames work, and how to mount one.
- **More sources.** TickTick; calendars through the same connections as lists (iCloud, Nextcloud and Fastmail by
  CalDAV, Outlook through Microsoft), which is also the way to adding and editing events.

## Later

- **Adding and editing events** at the wall.
- **A Home tab** through Home Assistant: lights and scenes beside the calendar.
- **Dinner** on the wall.
- **Reading the tablet's own calendars**, where someone has signed in on the device.
- **More photo sources**, such as Immich.
- **Screens other than 1920×1080 landscape.**

## Not planned

- A hosted Gingham service. Whoever runs a copy holds its secrets.
- Apple Reminders, Google Keep, AnyList, OurGroceries, Bring! and Cozi, which have no official way in.
